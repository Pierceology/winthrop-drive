Bringing Winthrop into view

Loading measured geography and aerial imagery…

A real geographic foundation.

Explore the whole peninsula or take a prototype drive on the mapped roads. This remains an incomplete city reconstruction, with no race modes yet.

Terrain & aerial image
MassGIS LiDAR elevation, sampled at approximately 7.3 m across town and 1 m around Yirrell Beach, with 2025 Massachusetts aerial imagery. City-wide imagery now has twice the previous linear resolution, split into four smaller textures. Shirley Street / Yirrell Beach uses a 4096-pixel local image patch, approximately 24 cm per output pixel. Elevation is shown at true scale.
Photographed buildings
How the houses are made
Assessor photographs are individually aligned to visible wall planes on footprint-matched houses. Clear wall sections can be registered separately, preserving their location within a facade. Trees, deep porches, uncertain address matches and complex wings can leave a house or wall pending. These are partial photographic reconstructions, not complete scans. Photographs load around the camera as you drive. Click an address to visit or review a house.
Roads
MassDOT centerlines and available surface widths. Road surfaces follow source widths; missing widths use an approximate 6 m. At driving distance, pavement uses crisp meter-scaled asphalt detail; asphalt remains sharp at every viewing distance. Estimated curb and sidewalk geometry now follows the photographed Point Shirley block, with gaps at mapped junctions. These details are not surveyed. Rounded pavement joins close gaps at bends and junctions. Pavement and driving sample the rendered terrain surface. Cyan lines optionally show centerlines. Driving is constrained to these corridors. At road edges the car is gently guided along the drivable corridor; automatic teleporting is removed. This is not a traffic-law simulation. This historical inventory may differ from recent construction.
Boundary
Winthrop's Census boundary, extended south to include all of Deer Island. Surrounding East Boston and Revere buildings are excluded. The two mainland connections are Belle Isle Bridge and Winthrop Parkway.
Audio & radio
Engine and road sounds respond to driving. Harbor FM and Coast AM are original synthesized instrumental loops, not live stations. Your music plays local files from your device; files are not uploaded. Engine and radio start off. Pause, leaving the drive, or hiding the page stops playback.
Street inventory
Selectable source-linked survey markers: 565 utility poles, 23 stop points and 9 hydrants from OpenStreetMap. Mapped poles receive approximate physical models only where they clear the current road and building geometry; conflicting locations are withheld. Nine hydrants have approximate physical models. The optional markers retain source links. A separate MassDOT inventory supplies 118 legible sign records, including 24 STOP, 4 DO NOT ENTER, 4 ONE WAY and a Revere Street guide sign. Their recorded coordinates and compass orientations are used; mounting dimensions are interpreted as inches and panel artwork approximates the recorded legend. Historical records do not establish current completeness. Click a sign for its inventory date and source.
Tree scenery
Reusable Kenney Nature Kit models (CC0) fill mapped vegetation areas in Explore and Drive. 27 crown centers were visually screened against 2025 Massachusetts aerial imagery. A further 4,526 architectural placements come from 2021 NOAA canopy areas hosted by MassEOEEA, with road, building and low-ground exclusions. Those placements are scenery instances, not measured individual trees. Trunk positions, heights, species and crown shapes remain estimates; the area layer omits many isolated street trees. Optional inspection markers expose the individually reviewed references.
Living world
Animated water, 12 ambient cars and 10 walkers. One-way directions are spatially matched from OpenStreetMap, not a verified municipal traffic inventory. Auto cruising and ambient cars use matched roads only; unmatched directions remain unknown. On two-way roads, spawn and cruise use the right-hand lane with blended turns. Single-lane one-way roads use the road center. Cruise stops when a mapped continuation ends. Traffic and pedestrian behavior are prototypes: walkers use estimated roadside routes, with building avoidance. They are not measured sidewalk routes. Street names are navigation overlays. The 66 mapped place labels come from OpenStreetMap and are not a complete or verified-current business directory. Click a label for its source.
Vehicle performance & cameras
Ferrari mode targets the 458 Italia’s 325 km/h (202 mph) top speed and under-3.4-second 0–100 km/h performance, using simplified game physics rather than a full drivetrain simulation. Camera cycles Chase, Hood and Interior; the interior view has a simplified dashboard. Ferrari specifications.
House checklist
Review available residential assessor addresses by street, including unit entries. Approvals and Needs work flags save to your signed-in account. Labels appear above nearby matched buildings in exploration and driving. Parcel-only locations and estimated exteriors are identified; this is not a complete current municipal address directory.
Evening drive & garage
Evening mode dims the sky, lights selected residential windows and switches on two road-following headlights. Window occupancy is atmospheric, not real household data. Choose the Ferrari or simpler sedan and wagon models, and customize body paint.
Coastal flyover
A one-click, 70-second cinematic camera tour through the residential block, Yirrell Beach and Deer Island. Stop any time or drive from the viewed area.
Driving assistance
Road-edge contact gently steers and slides the car back into the mapped corridor. Only the Reset car button repositions it. Wrong-way travel shows a warning without freezing the car. Short terrain bumps are filtered consistently for the road and vehicle. These are playability improvements, not a surveyed road-grade reconstruction.
Batch inspection
View the source photographs beside three angles of every photo-covered house. Inspection renders use the same geometry as the game. Fifteen scripted cruise starts are checked for road departure, building-center overlap and unexpected resets. This does not certify full-scene graphics or exact house dimensions.
Reference review
Compare the house models with their photographs. The CPU inspection view uses the same geometry and photo surfaces as the driving game. Full-street photographic reconstruction remains unfinished; races are deferred while driving behavior is refined.
